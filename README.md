Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d` — one line, at most 200
characters, about a small good thing that happened today. The warmest, most
surprising line takes the $5.

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single original sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

> This morning the ojek driver who dropped me off refused my tip, then
> pressed a warm pisang goreng into my hand and said the rain had already
> paid him.

150 characters, one sentence, one concrete detail (a warm pisang goreng, a
morning ride in the rain), and a turn at the end the reader does not see
coming. Before writing, the program checks its own output against the
competition gate: 1 to 200 characters, not only white space, a single line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

No network, no dependencies, no secrets. The output is identical on every
run.
